# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and constructive community. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and abusive conduct are not accepted.

Discuss ideas and code on their merits, assume good intent while verifying claims, protect user privacy, and make space for people at different experience levels. Maintainers may remove harmful content or participation. Report conduct concerns privately through the project maintainers rather than posting sensitive details publicly.
